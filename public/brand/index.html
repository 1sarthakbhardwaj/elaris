Clear space

One E on every side

E is the height of the letterform inside the icon — 53% of the icon's full height. Keep at least that much space clear on all four sides, in every lockup.

Minimum sizes

Small, but not smaller

Below 16px the three nodes close up. Use the icon, never the lockup, at those sizes. In print the icon holds to 5mm and the horizontal lockup to 30mm.

Typography

Three open-source faces, each with one job. Bricolage Grotesque speaks, Geist explains, Geist Mono labels. A face never does another face's job.
