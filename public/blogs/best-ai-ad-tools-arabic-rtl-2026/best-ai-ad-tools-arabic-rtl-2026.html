How we judged

Almost every tool in this category says it supports Arabic. Most of them mean the text field accepts Arabic characters. That is not the same thing as producing an Arabic ad.

So the primary criterion here is layout mirroring: when the language changes, does the composition flip so the logo, the reading order and the call to action move to the side an Arabic reader expects, or does the tool paste translated words into an English layout? Everything else, price, video, publishing, media buying, is secondary on this page, and we have said plainly where each tool beats us on it.

Why the mirroring criterion matters more than the feature list

A translated ad and a mirrored ad look identical in a spreadsheet of capabilities. They do not look identical on a phone in Doha.

In a translated ad, the logo stays in the top left, so the first thing an Arabic reader sees is the empty corner. The call to action stays on the left, so the eye finishes the ad and finds nothing. If the Arabic has been set in a Latin typeface, the letters do not join, which reads roughly like English printed with a space between every letter.

None of that is caught by a translation review, because the words are correct. It is caught by a layout review, and most teams do not run one for a market they do not read.

How to choose

If Arabic is a market you actually sell in, start with the mirroring question and let it filter the list before anything else does. Most of the tools here are good at what they were built for, and none of them were built for that.

If Arabic is a nice-to-have and your real problem is media buying, enterprise governance or price, the honest answer is that Smartly, Celtra or Canva respectively will serve you better than we will, and the rows above say so.

Frequently asked

What is the best AI ad tool for Arabic creative?

For Arabic specifically, the deciding capability is automatic layout mirroring, and ElarisLabs is the only tool in this comparison that does it from one approved master: the composition flips, the Arabic typeface comes from the brand kit, the copy is retypeset and safe zones are recalculated per placement. Tools such as Canva and Adobe Express support Arabic text but require a manual rebuild for each mirrored size, and the enterprise platforms handle Arabic through templates a team builds per market.

Do Canva or Adobe Express support right-to-left ad layouts?

Both support Arabic text and both let a designer build a right-to-left layout by hand. Neither publishes an automatic mirroring feature that takes an approved English composition and produces the Arabic equivalent across sizes. For one or two assets this is a non-issue. For a campaign across eleven placements in two languages, it is the whole cost.

What is the cheapest AI ad creative tool in 2026?

Among the tools here, Adobe Express Premium at $9.99/mo and Canva Pro at about $12/mo are the cheapest paid tiers, with Pencil Core at $14/mo close behind. Canva, Adobe Express, Creatify and ElarisLabs all have free tiers. Cheapest per month and cheapest per finished ad are different questions: at volume, the cost that matters is how many assets a person has to touch by hand.

Which AI ad tools publish their pricing?

AdCreative.ai, Canva, Adobe Express, Creatify, Pencil and ElarisLabs publish list pricing. Smartly.io, Celtra, Bannerflow and The Brief are quote-based, which usually signals an enterprise sales motion with a floor well above the self-serve tiers.

Can one tool cover both creative production and media buying?

Smartly.io is the clearest example in this set, and that combination is its main advantage. Most other tools, ElarisLabs included, stop at the finished asset and hand off to the buying platform. Whether the combination is worth it depends on whether your creative and media budgets are actually held by the same team.
