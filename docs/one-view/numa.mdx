Numa is the AI analyst inside One View Dashboard. Ask it about any brand's results, such as "Which platform drove the cheapest leads last week?", and it answers from your synced data, not from guesses. Each answer comes with a table of the numbers it used and, where it helps, a chart.

Numa is for team members. Clients and share links don't include it.

## Where to ask

- The box on **Home**.
- **Elaris AI** in the left rail, with your chat history alongside.
- **Ask Numa** in the top bar, or press **⌘J**, from any page.
- Type a question into search (**⌘K**) for quick answers on how to do something in the app.

## What it can look up

Numa works only from what it can look up in your workspace:

- the brands and dashboards you have,
- any metric for any period, channel or campaign,
- comparisons between two periods,
- data health, such as which accounts are stale, and
- what each metric means and how it's worked out.

Turn on **Thinking** for harder analysis. It takes longer, and Numa can switch it on by itself when a question needs it.

## Teach it your way of working

Under **Elaris AI → Context**:

- **Instructions** are what Numa should always know, such as house rules, naming conventions or the KPIs each client cares about.
- **Skills** are saved prompts you run by typing **/** and the skill's name, so a report you ask for every week takes one line.

## Scheduled jobs

**Elaris AI → Scheduled jobs** answers a question every day, week or month, for example a Monday summary of last week's spend and ROAS for each brand. Each run is saved to **Chat history**; it isn't emailed.

Chat history, instructions and skills are shared by the whole team, so everyone builds on the same context.

## Use your data from Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor

**Elaris AI → MCP** creates a token for your workspace and shows how to add Elaris Analytics to Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor and other MCP clients. Your assistant can then ask the same questions of the same data. Keep the token private: anyone who has it can read your workspace's data.
