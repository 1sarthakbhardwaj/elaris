## Supported platforms

| Platform | How you connect |
| --- | --- |
| Google Analytics 4 | Google sign-in, or a service account |
| Google Ads | Google sign-in |
| Meta Ads | Meta sign-in (lasts about 60 days, then sign in again) |
| TikTok Ads | TikTok sign-in |
| Display & Video 360 | Google sign-in, then pick a report: Standard, Reach & frequency or YouTube |
| Snapchat Ads | Snapchat sign-in |
| LinkedIn Ads | LinkedIn sign-in |
| X Ads | Paste your X API keys |
| Pinterest Ads | Pinterest sign-in |
| Google Sheets | Google sign-in, or a service account. Use it for numbers you keep in a sheet |

Google Analytics 4 brings sessions and key events but no spend, so it never counts towards money metrics such as spend or ROAS.

## Connect an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Open **Data → Data sources** and press **Connect data source**, or use **Connect a data source** from a dashboard's data sources menu to add it to that dashboard straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the platform">
    Choose the platform from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    If the platform is already set up, press **Continue**. Otherwise press **Continue with** Google, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn or Pinterest and sign in. For X, choose **Enter keys instead**, paste the keys, press **Test connection**, then **Save and continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account">
    Choose the account from the list or paste its ID. You can rename the data source to anything you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how much history">
    Import the last **30 days**, **3 months**, **6 months** or **12 months**. You can pull more history later from the data source's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Press **Connect data source**. Its page opens while the data imports. If you started from a dashboard, it's already on that dashboard; otherwise add it to any dashboard in **Edit dashboard → Fields & filters**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
For Display & Video 360, connect the same advertiser once for each report you need. **Standard** brings spend, revenue, impressions, clicks and conversions. **Reach & frequency** brings unique reach each day and for the month so far, with no spend. **YouTube** repeats the spend, impressions and clicks of YouTube line items that Standard already counts, so don't add both into one total.
</Tip>

If an account you expect is missing from the list, the person who signed in can't see it. For DV360 and Snapchat, every person's sign-in is kept, so ask someone who can see the account to sign in too, and its accounts are added.

## What Elaris can access

Elaris reads your reporting data and never changes your campaigns.

- Google Analytics 4, Google Sheets, Meta, LinkedIn, Pinterest and TikTok are connected with read-only reporting access.
- Google Ads and DV360 ask for Google's standard advertising permission, because Google doesn't offer a read-only option for them. Elaris only reads reports with it.
- Sign-ins and keys are stored encrypted and are never shown again after you save them.

## How often data refreshes

Every night, around 02:00 UTC, Elaris imports every connected account again. It re-fetches the **last 30 days** each time, because ad platforms keep updating conversions for days after a click. Recent numbers can therefore move a little from one morning to the next.

Data always runs up to **yesterday**. To refresh sooner:

- **Run sync now** on Data health re-fetches every account.
- **Sync this client** on a dashboard re-fetches that brand's accounts.
- **Re-import now**, in a data source's **⋯** menu, re-fetches one account.

## Keeping connections healthy

**Data → Data health** shows the accounts that are syncing, freshness alerts, access still pending, recent imports and each connector's state. Accounts that need you are listed under **Needs attention**. An account counts as stale when it hasn't imported for 36 hours.

To sign in again or rotate a key, open **Data sources → Connectors**, press **Edit credentials** (or **Connect**) on the platform's row, sign in or paste the new credentials, press **Test connection**, then **Save and continue**.

## Rename a data source

On **Data sources**, hover the row and press the pencil beside its name, or choose **Rename** in the row's **⋯** menu. Type any name and press **Save name**. The platform's account ID doesn't change.
