## Create a dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Open **Report → Dashboards** and press **Create dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick its currency">
    Give it the brand's name and choose its reporting currency. Elaris fetches the brand's logo for you. The currency can't be changed later, so pick the one the client reports in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add charts">
    The dashboard opens empty and in edit mode. Press **Add chart** for each chart you want, then **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect its data">
    Use the dashboard's data sources menu → **Connect a data source** to connect an account straight onto it, or add accounts you've already connected in **Edit dashboard → Fields & filters**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Dashboards** page lists every brand in the workspace, as cards or a list, with a 30-day spend trend, spend and ROAS. Search it or filter by status.

## The four tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your own grid of charts, laid out for this brand |
| **Channel detail** | One channel at a time: its KPIs, daily trend, campaigns and accounts |
| **Creative** | Every ad ranked by spend, with impressions, CTR, conversions and cost per conversion, plus the gap between your best and worst cost per conversion |
| **Cross-channel** | Twelve metrics side by side for every channel |

## Dates and filters

Pick **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days** above the dashboard, **Last 14 days** under **More**, or choose custom dates. Data always ends yesterday.

**Filters** narrow the view to channels, campaigns, ad groups and ads. Under **Campaigns**, tick campaigns one by one, or type text such as `PMax` and press **All campaigns containing "PMax"** to keep every campaign with it in the name, including ones that start later. The filters are part of the page address, so you can bookmark a filtered view.

A notice appears at the top when something affects the numbers: an account has stopped syncing, accounts report in different timezones, or spend was converted from another currency.

## Lay out the Overview

Open the **Overview** tab and press **Edit dashboard**, then **Add chart** and pick a type:

| Group | Chart types |
| --- | --- |
| KPIs | Scorecard, Scoreboard, Metric breakdown |
| Time series | Line, Combo, Stacked bar, Trend by channel |
| Comparisons | Bar, Pie, Channel ranking |
| Tables | Table, Pivot table |
| Text & images | Text, Image |

- **Move and resize:** drag a chart's move handle to put it anywhere on the grid, and drag its corner to change its size. A dashboard holds up to 40 charts.
- **Chart settings:** click a chart to set its metric, the field to group by and how many rows to show. Any chart can have its own filters and date range.
- **Scorecards** can show a trend line and compare with the previous period, the same period last year, or nothing.
- **Fields & filters** sets what feeds the whole dashboard: its data sources (from any brand), channels, rules on any field, and the fields its charts offer. In a rule such as **Campaign contains**, type the text and press Enter; the line under the box shows which campaigns it covers.

Press **Save** when you're done. Everyone with access sees the new layout.

## Dashboard settings

Open a dashboard's **Settings** for:

- **Branding:** the logo, display name, monogram and contact details your client sees. See [Share with clients](/one-view/sharing#branding).
- **Connected accounts:** the accounts feeding this dashboard, with a checklist of any access the client still has to grant.
- **Share links:** links that open the dashboard without a sign-in.
- **Move to workspace:** move the brand to another workspace.
