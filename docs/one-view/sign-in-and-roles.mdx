## Signing in

Open [analytics.elarislabs.ai](https://analytics.elarislabs.ai/login) and choose one of:

- **Continue with Google**, with the Google account your invitation was sent to.
- **Continue with ElarisLabs**, where it's offered: the same email and password you use for Creative Studio. To reset that password, use **Forgot password**, which takes you to Creative Studio.

You stay signed in for 30 days. Your access is checked on every request, so a removed person loses access straight away.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Client** | View only: the dashboards, or whole workspaces, they have been given. No Numa and no data settings. |
| **Team** | Everything a client can, across every dashboard, plus connect data, build and edit dashboards, use Numa, share and export. |
| **Super admin** | Everything a team member can, plus manage **Members** and delete dashboards. |

## Inviting people

Only super admins can invite people.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **More → Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite by email">
    Enter the person's email address and choose their role. For a client, choose which dashboards or workspaces they can see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    The invitation arrives by email. If email isn't set up, copy the invite link and send it yourself. The link works for 14 days, and the person must sign in with the email address it was sent to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What clients see

A client signs in to **Your dashboards**, which lists only the dashboards shared with them. If they have just one, it opens straight away. They can change dates and filters and download data, but they can't edit charts, connect data or use Numa.

To share a dashboard without creating a sign-in, send a [share link](/one-view/sharing#share-links) instead.

## Workspaces

A workspace is a set of dashboards with its own brands. Click the workspace name in the top bar, next to Elaris Analytics, to:

- switch to another workspace,
- create a workspace, or
- rename the current one.

To move a brand, open its dashboard **Settings** and press **Move to workspace**. Platform sign-ins are shared by every workspace, so you don't sign in to Meta or Google again for each one.

## Your profile

Open **More → My profile**, or click your photo at the top right, to change or remove your photo, see your role and switch between light and dark mode.
