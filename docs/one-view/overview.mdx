One View Dashboard is **Elaris Analytics**, the ElarisLabs reporting hub at [analytics.elarislabs.ai](https://analytics.elarislabs.ai). Each brand gets its own dashboard, built from every ad platform it runs on. Your client can open it as their own portal instead of waiting for a monthly deck.

## What you can do with it

- **See every channel in one place.** Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, Display & Video 360, Snapchat, LinkedIn, X and Pinterest, plus Google Analytics 4 and Google Sheets.
- **Compare fairly.** Every platform is mapped to one set of metrics in one currency, and rates such as CTR and ROAS are worked out from the totals, so the columns line up.
- **Build the view each client needs.** Fourteen chart types on a drag-and-drop grid, each with its own filters and dates.
- **Find your best ads.** The Creative tab ranks every ad by spend, with its CTR, conversions and cost per conversion.
- **Ask Numa.** The AI analyst answers questions about any brand's results from your synced data.
- **Share it.** Give a client their own sign-in, or send a link that opens without one.
- **Take the data anywhere.** Download any chart or table, or send data to Google Sheets, Cloud Storage or a BI tool on a schedule.

## How it fits together

| Level | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace | A set of dashboards with its own brands. Switch or create workspaces from the top bar. |
| Dashboard | One brand's reporting: its charts, reporting currency, branding and share links. |
| Data source | One connected ad account, analytics property or sheet. A dashboard can draw on several. |

Platform sign-ins are shared by every workspace, so you sign in to each ad platform once.

## Finding your way around

The left rail has six areas:

| Area | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A box to ask Numa anything, your recent dashboards and the state of the imports |
| **Elaris AI** | Chats with Numa, chat history, scheduled jobs, MCP, and Numa's instructions and skills |
| **Data** | Data Explorer, Data health, Data sources, Dimensions, Metrics and Currencies |
| **Report** | Dashboards and Share links |
| **Export** | Scheduled exports to Google Sheets, Cloud Storage or a CSV link |
| **More** | About (with keyboard shortcuts), My profile and Members |

The top bar holds the workspace switcher, search (**⌘K** or **/**), **Ask Numa** (**⌘J**), the time of the last import and the light or dark mode switch.
