## Data Explorer

**Data → Data Explorer** puts any dimension against any metric, across every data source. The toolbar reads left to right:

1. **Dimensions** and **Metrics**: for example, spend and conversions by campaign.
2. **The date range**.
3. **Add filter**: rules on any dimension (such as **Campaign contains** "Ramadan"), rules on metrics (such as **Spend is greater than** 100) and a row limit of up to 5,000 rows. Press **Apply** to use them.
4. **Currency**: the currency to show money in.

**Export**, at the top right, downloads the result as CSV, TSV or Excel, or sends it to a new Google Sheet in your own Drive.

## Download a chart or table

Every chart and table, on dashboards and share links, has a **⋯** menu with **Download CSV** and **Download Excel**.

## Scheduled exports

Send data out on a schedule, so a spreadsheet or BI report stays up to date without anyone pulling it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the export">
    Open **Export** in the left rail and press **Create export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a destination">
    Choose **Google Sheets**, **Google Cloud Storage**, or a **private CSV link** that Excel, Power BI, Looker Studio or Tableau can read. Connect Google or Google Cloud when asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the data">
    Choose the metrics and dimensions, the date range and any filters, then press **Preview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and schedule it">
    Name it, choose **Daily**, **Mondays** or **Manual**, and press **Save and run**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Scheduled exports run every morning after the nightly import, so they carry the freshest numbers.

<Note>
Treat a private CSV link like a password: anyone who has it can read that export.
</Note>
