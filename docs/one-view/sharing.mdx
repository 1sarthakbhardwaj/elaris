There are two ways to show a client their results:

| | Client sign-in | Share link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How they open it** | They sign in with Google or an ElarisLabs account | A link that opens without signing in |
| **What they see** | Every dashboard you've given them, under **Your dashboards** | One dashboard, with the tabs you choose |
| **How long it lasts** | Until you remove their access | 180 days, or until you revoke it |
| **Set it up in** | **More → Members** (super admins) | The dashboard's **Settings → Share links** |

Either way, a client sees only their own dashboards, never another client's. They can change dates and filters and download CSV or Excel, but they can't edit charts, connect data or use Numa.

For client sign-ins, see [Sign-in, roles and workspaces](/one-view/sign-in-and-roles#inviting-people).

## Share links

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard's settings">
    Open the client's dashboard and go to **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a link">
    Under **Share links**, press **New link**, give it a label and pick the tabs it includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Copy the link and send it to your client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Links expire after 180 days. You can revoke a link at any time and restore it later, and each link shows how many times it has been opened. **Report → Share links** lists every live link in the workspace.

## See what your client sees

A client's dashboard looks exactly the same to you as it does to them. To check a share link itself, press **Client view** on the dashboard, which opens their link in a new tab.

## Branding

Make each dashboard look like the client's own:

- **Logo:** Elaris fetches the brand's logo when you create the dashboard. Click the logo on the dashboard to upload a different one or remove it.
- **Settings → Branding:** set the display name, a monogram of up to three letters, and the contact name and email your client sees, then press **Save branding**.

Shared pages carry a small "Prepared by ElarisLabs AI" line.
