## Visibility

**The share of answers that name your brand.** If your non-branded prompts were asked 120 times this week across all engines and runs, and 30 of those answers named you as recommended or mentioned, your visibility is 25%.

- It counts non-branded prompts unless you choose otherwise, because those show whether AI picks you when someone is deciding.
- **Current** is the last 7 days. The change compares it with the 7 days before.
- You can break it down by engine, topic, tag, language and prompt.

## Share of voice

**Your share of all mentions among you and your primary competitors.** If across this week's answers your brand was named 40 times and your four competitors 160 times between them, your share of voice is 20%. Where visibility asks “how often are we there?”, share of voice asks “how much of the conversation is ours?”.

## Average position

**When you are named, where in the answer you appear.** 1 means first. A brand named in every answer but always last (position 5) is in a weaker spot than one named slightly less often but usually first.

## Citation rate

**The share of answers that link to your own website.** Being cited means AI used your page as a source, which tends to keep you in the answer as models are updated.

## Brand injection

**The share of the AI's own searches that add your brand name unprompted.** When someone asks for “the best sofa shop” and the AI searches for “Lunebrook sofas”, it already associates you with the topic. It is shown on the Fanouts page.

## Weighted score

**One visibility figure across engines, each weighted by how much it matters to you.** The default weights reflect how many people use each engine:

| Engine | Default weight |
| --- | --- |
| Google AI Overviews | 30 |
| ChatGPT | 30 |
| Gemini | 15 |
| Google AI Mode | 10 |
| Perplexity | 10 |
| Others | 5 each |

Only engines with answers in the period count, and their weights are rescaled to add up to 100%. Change them in the market's settings; the dashboard shows the weights in use.

## Accuracy

Statements AI makes about your brand are checked against the facts you keep in your fact sheet (prices, locations, delivery, policies). Each claim is marked:

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong | Disagrees with a fact, such as a wrong price |
| Outdated | Repeats a fact past its valid-until date, such as an old offer |
| Unverified | Not covered by your fact sheet, so it can't be checked yet |
| Accurate | Matches a fact you keep |

## 95% ranges

Every rate is shown with the range it could plausibly be, given how many answers it rests on. 50% from 8 answers might really be anywhere from about 22% to 78%; 50% from 300 answers is between about 44% and 56%. The fewer answers, the wider the range.

<Note>
Before acting on a change, check whether the ranges overlap. If this week is 30% (22–39%) and last week was 26% (18–35%), the difference may be noise. More runs per prompt, or a longer date range, tighten the ranges.
</Note>

## Filters
