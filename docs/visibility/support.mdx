## Frequently asked questions

### Why did my visibility change when I didn't do anything?

AI answers change as models are updated and as they read new pages, including your competitors'. Check the 95% ranges first: if this period's and the last overlap, the move may be noise. Then open the topic or prompt that moved and read the answers to see what changed. See [95% ranges](/visibility/metrics#95%25-ranges).

### Why does ChatGPT in Elaris say something different from what I see?

Answers vary between askings, and depend on country, language and whether you're signed in with history. Elaris asks from your market's country, without personal history, several times per prompt, which is why it reports rates rather than single answers.

### How often is data collected?

Each prompt is asked three times on each engine every two days by default. Admins can change both in the market's settings, or run collection straight away with Run now.

### Why is a brand missing when the answer clearly names it?

It may be written differently, for example in another script such as Arabic, or as a short name. Add an alias for the brand in Settings → Market and new answers will match it.

### Can I track more than one country?

Yes. Add a region in Settings → Collection. It copies your brands, topics and prompts (rewriting the ones that name your first country for the new one) and has its own dashboard.

### Which AI bots does AI Traffic recognise?

Agents and crawlers from OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Mistral, Apple, Amazon, DuckDuckGo, ByteDance, Common Crawl and Cohere, among others. See [AI traffic](/visibility/ai-traffic) for how they are grouped.

### Does connecting Vercel or Cloudflare slow down my site?

No. Vercel sends logs after each request is served, and Cloudflare is read from analytics; neither adds anything to the path of a request.

## Contact

- Questions about Elaris Visibility, your account or a connection: [sales@elarislabs.ai](mailto:sales@elarislabs.ai)
- Privacy and data requests: [privacy@elarislabs.ai](mailto:privacy@elarislabs.ai)

Please include your workspace name and, for a connection problem, whether it's Vercel or Cloudflare and any message you saw.

## Your data

- From request logs and analytics, Elaris keeps only requests made by AI agents and crawlers. Requests from people are discarded as they arrive and never stored.
- The access you grant to Vercel or Cloudflare is stored encrypted and used only to set up and read AI traffic for your workspace.
- Only people invited to your workspace can see its data, at the role they were given.
- Disconnecting removes Elaris's access on your side straight away. Data already collected stays in your workspace; to have it deleted, write to privacy@elarislabs.ai.

Read the full [privacy policy](https://www.elarislabs.ai/privacy-policy) and [terms](https://www.elarislabs.ai/terms).
