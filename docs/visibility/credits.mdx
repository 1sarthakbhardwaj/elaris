## Credits

Credits are how usage is counted: **one credit is one AI answer**. Every plan includes enough credits for all its prompts on its schedule, with room left for Ask Elaris, so most teams never need to think about them.

| What | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| One answer from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Perplexity or Copilot | 1 |
| One answer from Claude or Grok (Enterprise) | 3 |
| An Ask Elaris question | 1 |
| An Ask Elaris question with Think | 2 |
| AI traffic, Search Console, Analytics, page checks and exports | None |

Credits are counted from the answers that come back, so a failed or skipped answer costs nothing.

## Asking more often

**Settings → Market → Sampling** sets how many times each prompt is asked per run and how often runs happen. Asking more often than your plan includes uses more credits. A month of tracking uses about **prompts × engines × runs per prompt × runs a month**: for example, 550 prompts on 3 engines, asked once a day (30 runs), use 49,500 credits.

**Settings → Plan and credits** works this out from your current settings and warns you when it is more than your plan includes. When you change plan, every region moves to the new plan's schedule and keeps the engines it picked, up to the number the plan asks.

## When credits run out

A run only starts when the month's credits cover all of it, so you never get half a run. If they don't, collection pauses, Settings → Plan and credits says how many the run needs, and it starts again when credits are added or the month resets. Nothing you have collected is lost, and every report stays available. Ask Elaris pauses too.

To use fewer credits, ask each prompt less often (Settings → Market → Sampling), track fewer engines, or archive prompts you no longer need.

## Months, top-ups and the trial

- Credits reset on the first day of each month (UTC) and don't roll over.
- Top-ups add credits for the current month. Ask from Settings → Plan and credits → Add credits; they are usually added the same day.
- The trial lasts 7 days, with 100 prompts on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini. When you set up your workspace yourself, you pick Starter or Growth and add a card on Stripe's secure page, and the trial starts then: nothing is charged until it ends, we email you 3 days before, and the plan you picked starts on day 8. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. A workspace ElarisLabs sets up starts its trial when it's created; choose a plan from Settings → Plan and credits to keep collecting.
- One free trial per person and card. A second workspace, or a card that already had a trial, starts its plan straight away.
