## Why AI visibility matters

More and more buying decisions start with a question to an AI assistant: “What's the best sofa shop in Doha?”, “Which project management tool suits a small agency?”. The answer usually names three to five brands. If yours isn't one of them, you aren't in the running, and unlike a search results page there is no second page to scroll to.

AI answers are also hard to see from the outside. They change from one asking to the next, differ between ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answers, and depend on the country and language of the person asking. Elaris Visibility measures them properly, so you can track them like any other channel.

## How Elaris works, in short

1. **Ask the questions your customers ask.** You choose the topics that matter to your business. Elaris turns real Google searches into natural questions (prompts), in each language you track.
2. **Ask every engine, repeatedly, from your market.** Each prompt is asked to each AI engine several times, from the country you target, on a regular schedule.
3. **Read every answer.** Elaris records which brands are named and how (recommended or merely mentioned), in what order, how they are described, which web pages the answer cites, and what the AI searched for before answering.
4. **Turn it into numbers and next steps.** Visibility, share of voice, position, sentiment and accuracy, each with a range that shows how certain it is, plus the content gaps and websites worth working on.

The details are in [How it works](/visibility/how-it-works), and every number is defined in [Metrics](/visibility/metrics).

## What you can do with it

- **Know where you stand:** how often each brand is named for your topics, on each engine, in each country, and whether that is rising or falling.
- **Find out why:** read the actual answers, see which websites AI relied on, and which searches it ran.
- **Fix what AI gets wrong:** claims that contradict your own facts (prices, locations, policies) are flagged by severity.
- **Win topics:** see the questions where competitors are named and you aren't, write a content brief for them, and build a list of websites to get mentioned on.
- **See AI on your own site:** which AI agents and crawlers read your pages, and whether new pages get picked up.
- **See what it sends you:** with Search Console and Analytics connected, each page's impressions, clicks and visits from AI assistants sit next to how often AI cites it.
