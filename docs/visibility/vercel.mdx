description: "For sites hosted on Vercel. Connecting adds a log drain that sends your project's request logs to Elaris, which keeps only AI agents and crawlers."

## Before you start

- Your Vercel team is on **Pro or Enterprise**. Vercel offers log drains only on those plans; on Hobby, use [Cloudflare](/visibility/cloudflare) or send logs by hand.
- You are an **admin** of the Elaris workspace, and can install integrations on the Vercel team that owns your site.
- Your website is set in Elaris (**Settings → General**), so the right project is picked for you.

## Connect

<Frame caption="Settings → Integrations: the Website traffic panel, where Connect Vercel is.">
  <img src="/images/visibility/integrations-traffic.jpg" width="1536" height="564" alt="Integrations: connect Vercel or Cloudflare for AI traffic" />
</Frame>

1. In Elaris Visibility, open **Settings → Integrations** and select **Connect Vercel**.
2. Vercel opens and asks you to install the **Elaris Visibility** integration. Choose the team that owns your site. When asked which projects to share, pick your site's project (or all projects, if you prefer to choose in Elaris).
3. You come back to Elaris. If one project serves your website's domain, the drain is created for it straight away. If several could, tick the ones that make up your site (for example the main site and a shop on a subdomain) and select **Connect**.
4. The Vercel panel now shows **Connected to** your team and the projects included. AI traffic appears on the AI Traffic page within a few minutes of the next visit from an AI bot.

## What happens behind the scenes

Elaris creates one **log drain** on your team named “Elaris Visibility: AI traffic”, covering only the projects you chose. Vercel then sends each request log to your workspace's own endpoint, signed with a secret unique to your workspace, so nobody else can send data in your name. Elaris keeps the requests from AI agents and crawlers, checks them against the IP addresses their vendors publish, and discards the rest straight away.

Nothing changes on your site: no code, no redirects, no added latency. You can see the drain in Vercel under your team's settings, in Drains.

## What Elaris can access

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Drains (read and write) | To create the drain for your project, and remove it when you disconnect |
| Projects (read) | To list your projects and their domains, so the right one is picked |
| Team and user (read) | To show which team is connected |
| Integration configuration (read and write) | To remove the integration from your team when you disconnect |

Elaris can't deploy, change environment variables, edit domains or read your code. The access token Vercel issues is stored encrypted and is used only to manage the drain.

## Changing or disconnecting

- **Add or change projects:** select **Connect Vercel** again and pick the projects. The new drain replaces the old one, so logs aren't counted twice.
- **Disconnect:** in **Settings → Integrations**, select **Disconnect** on the Vercel panel (click twice to confirm). Elaris removes the drain and the integration from your team. AI traffic already collected stays in Elaris.
- **From Vercel's side:** removing the integration under your team's Integrations removes its drain too.

## Troubleshooting

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Vercel only sends logs from Pro and Enterprise teams” | The team is on Hobby. Upgrade it, or connect through Cloudflare instead. |
| “The integration can't see any projects” | Install the integration again and share your site's project with it. |
| A list of projects to choose from | More than one project serves your domain, or your website isn't set in Elaris. Tick the projects that make up your site. |
| “That connection timed out” | The choice wasn't made within 15 minutes. Select Connect Vercel again. |
| Connected, but no traffic | AI bots may not have visited yet; busy sites usually see some within an hour. Check that the drain is listed in Vercel under Drains and shows no errors. |

<Note>
Prefer to set it up yourself? Open **Set it up by hand instead** on the Vercel panel: it shows the endpoint and secret to paste into a drain you create in Vercel.
</Note>
